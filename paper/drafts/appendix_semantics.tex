\section{Benchmark-semantics audit: zero growth and a condition-state leak in the pool-v4-era battery}
\label{app:semantics}

\textbf{Scope.} This appendix documents two properties of the pool-v4-era battery semantics (the committed frozen frontier, all A0--A5 battery scores, and the plateau analysis), discovered by the A6 audit machinery on 2026-09-29. Both are verified against committed data; neither is patched retroactively --- the frozen record stands, and the corrected comparison is pre-registered separately (amendment 2026-09-29 14:35 IST, commit 8274f9f).

\textbf{1. Zero-growth production.} The pool-v4-era leg rule evaluates maximal product with the biomass flux pinned at $0.5 \times g_{\max}$; when $g_{\max} = 0$ the pin is non-binding and the leg records product synthesis at exactly zero growth. The plateau champions (A0, seed 260927) have $g_{\max} = 0.0$ in the reference condition on all three targets: they are catalytically active but non-viable as growing cells. A battery-exact replication reproduces their committed rows to twelve decimals (e.g.\ 14BDO nominal 13.454545454545496). Under the corrected v23c semantics, no-growth legs are zeroed, so these designs score $R_{\mathrm{tiered}} = 0.0$.

\textbf{2. Per-condition state leak.} The pool-v4-era row evaluator applies condition exchange bounds cumulatively within a genome row. The glycerol uptake bound (GLYCEROL leg) and the acetate uptake bound (ACETATE leg) persist into every later leg: the ACETATE leg itself is evaluated with glycerol co-feed, and all three perturbation legs are evaluated with glycerol \emph{and} acetate co-feed. Leg-by-leg verification on an A6 selection (14BDO, seed 260927): ACETATE 4.5937 (leaked) vs 2.6419 (fresh state); PERT$-20$\% 9.1239 vs 5.6660; PERT$+20$\% 11.3889 vs 7.9311; O$_2$ PERT$-50$\% 10.2564 vs 6.7986. This is the same defect class as the withdrawn A6 v1 runner, present in the committed battery evaluator. Mild-leg retentions above 1.0 in the plateau record (e.g.\ 1.39/1.72/1.55) are partly this leak.

\textbf{Consequence.} Cross-semantics comparisons are not biological orderings: the plateau maximum $1.277027$ and the A6 selections' $0.746$--$0.813$ live in different metrics. Within either metric the ordering is consistent and opposite (plateau wins in the pool-v4-era metric; A6 wins maximally in the corrected metric, where plateau champions score zero). The corrected re-score of all comparison sets under one semantics is pre-registered (commit 8274f9f) before any evaluation. Verification scope: zero growth was verified directly on the three A0 seed-260927 champions; other plateau members ($n = 475/686/462$) were not individually re-evaluated at audit time, though all plateau members tie exactly under the pool-v4-era metric.

\textbf{Self-correction as a result.} Both properties surfaced because the project audits its own machinery: every metric port is diffed against its locked reference, withdrawals are preserved, and independent fresh-process re-validation precedes any paper fold-in. The flaw is in the benchmark's history, not hidden by it.
