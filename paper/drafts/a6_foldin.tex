\subsection{A6: direct robust-fitness search lands below the plateau, and the audit exposes a benchmark-semantics flaw (amendment 2026-09-29 07:31 IST, locked before computation)}
\label{sec:a6}

A5 established that plateau-constrained selection does not beat plateau membership. The direct question remained: does searching \emph{directly} under the tiered robust objective find robust optima off the nominal plateau? A6 locked exactly that arm at pool v4: the identical $(\mu{+}\lambda)$ configuration (MU=24, LAM=48, 60 generations, five seeds, three targets), with fitness $0.5\,R_{\mathrm{pert}} + 0.5\,\mathrm{mean}_k\, v_{p,k}/F_k$ computed under the corrected v23c battery semantics against the frozen committed pool-v4 frontier $F_k$.

\textit{Execution record.} The first A6 runner dropped per-condition fresh state, leaking the acetate-uptake bound into subsequent legs (4 of 9 legs contaminated); the audit caught the defect before completion, the 4 completed and 1 partial runs were withdrawn with their caches preserved (results/withdrawn\_a6\_v1/), and the repaired runner was proven bitwise-identical to the v23c context-manager reference before any further evaluation (commit 1086a4c). All 15 runs then completed on hosted runners; every selection was re-validated by fresh-process independently written code (165/165 checks, $10^{-9}$ relative tolerance).

\textit{Results, verbatim.} Every target converged to a single fitness across all five seeds: 14BDO $0.746298$, isobutanol $0.778085$, lycopene $0.812866$ (lycopene seeds differ at $\sim$$10^{-11}$; statistically decidable, scientifically null). Against the locked comparison data (plateau maximum $R_{\mathrm{tiered}} = 1.277027$ on all three targets), all fifteen selections land strictly below the plateau maximum: relative gaps 41.6\% (14BDO), 39.1\% (isobutanol), 36.3\% (lycopene). The locked rules named WIN, TIE-BREAK-LEVEL, and NULL bands; none covers a below-plateau outcome, so we record it verbatim as a fourth case: \textbf{BELOW-PLATEAU, a negative result}. Nominal cost (locked secondary endpoint): A6 selections retain 50.5\%, 52.5\%, and 59.1\% of the plateau nominal flux.

\textit{Comparability finding (unsoftened).} The locked comparison crosses two benchmark semantics, and the gap is mostly semantic. The plateau champions do not grow: their maximum growth rate in the reference condition is exactly zero on all three targets (they are zero-growth production factories), which a battery-exact replication reproduces to twelve decimals against the committed battery record. The pool-v4-era battery scores such designs by evaluating product at zero growth, and additionally carries a per-condition state leak: glycerol and acetate uptake bounds set by the GLYCEROL and ACETATE legs persist into the ACETATE and all three perturbation legs, inflating them (e.g.\ a PERT$+20$\% leg at 11.3889 with the leak versus 7.9311 without). Under A6's locked leak-free semantics the plateau champions score $0.0$; under the pool-v4-era semantics the A6 selections score $1.020122$ / $1.057527$ / $1.066227$. The verdict ordering flips with the semantics, so the cross-semantics comparison as locked is not interpretable as a biological ordering. Appendix~\ref{app:semantics} gives the full audit.
